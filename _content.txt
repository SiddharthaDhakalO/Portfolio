4 · The Studio (about & skills)
Curator's statement (bio)
Siddhartha Dhakal is a frontend web developer based in Kathmandu, Nepal, and an Information Technology student with — in his own words — an insatiable curiosity for technology. His work centres on the front of the web: the part people actually see and touch, made clean, responsive and pleasant to use.
He works most fluently in HTML, CSS and JavaScript, with a growing command of React and the .NET / Blazor world, and designs his interfaces in Figma before building them. Since 2024 he has built frontends professionally at The High Innovations — from the company's own website to the screens of a live point-of-sale system.
Away from client work, he builds to learn: a Kanban board to understand state, a weather app to step into Flutter, small experiments that end up in the Archive. He is an active member of Code For Change, where he helps run hackathons and bootcamps and champions open-source culture. This museum is the latest of those experiments — a portfolio that behaves like a place.
